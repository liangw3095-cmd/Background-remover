# Background remover

Kom sends character sprites (and ID card exports) here to have backgrounds removed.
Keep replies to Kom short: what was sent and anything they need to decide. Skip internals and process. Use the `sprite-background-removal` skill and follow it as written: one `cutout.py` run, one look at the sheet, send the files. Don't read or rewrite the scripts for a normal batch.

- The skill's source of truth is `sprite-background-removal/` in this repo (`SKILL.md` + `scripts/`).
- After changing it, run `python3 tests/check_skill.py`, then package it for Kom with skill-creator's `package_skill.py` and send the `.skill` file so they can click "Save skill".
- The app allows only 5 images per message. If Kom says "more coming", wait for the rest and "go", then do one run over all of them (earlier uploads stay available).
- Hair the generator drew in the bg colour (e.g. Ilse Varga on mint): Kom counts the enclosed bg-coloured areas inside the hair as hair, including the strips along the outer sides of a bob. Background seen between the hair tips and the neck, collar or coat stays clear even when enclosed: filling it gives grey chunks between hair and body (Ilse: pockets beside the neck below the jaw in Neutral, Wary, Angry, Happy; a big wedge in Tired). Tell them apart by the source: a gap is flat, exact bg colour bounded by strand tips and the body; inner hair is a shaded off-bg tone or has strand lines. `tools/fill_hair.py` fills on a cutout; pass `--gap-seeds "x,y;x,y"` (a point in each gap) to keep gaps clear, then check every sprite's neck area over a dark backdrop, not just the worst one.
- Images Kom sends already transparent (cut by another tool) can carry pale halo remnants around the hair. Composite them onto flat green (40,250,90) and run `cutout.py` as usual; its edge pass darkens the remnants to the hair colour.
- Patterned bg in one hue (e.g. Vesna Kral's pink burst streaks): the corners hit the darker streaks, so `cutout.py` keys on the wrong colour. Run `tools/flatten_bg.py IN OUT.png` on each image first (it sets everything of the bg hue that touches the border to the main tone), then cut the flattened PNGs. Check open mouths afterwards: pink mouths on a pink key go olive and see-through; restore them from the source.
- "Match the outfit colour with the cover": sample the garment on the sprite and on the cover (median of its pixels), then run `tools/recolor_outfit.py CUTOUT OUT --from R,G,B --to R,G,B` on each finished cutout, once per garment colour (shading and plaid carry over). Examples: Margot Whitlock red blazer to navy 35,48,75; Sol Reyes tank 155,1,2 to olive 76,78,49; Tamsin Lau plaid 114,13,23 to purple 97,68,113 (--tol 0.22 --min 800, then leftover 190,47,58 to 162,113,188 and 94,4,23 to 80,56,93). Sample the cover away from its background: Tamsin's cover cuff first read orange because the orange bg got into the sample (real tan: lit 196,161,139, shadow 140,103,90). For a part with a lit and a shadow tone use `tools/recolor_shaded.py` so the shadow follows (Tamsin cuffs: --light 210,141,145 --shadow 144,79,88 --to-light 196,161,139 --to-shadow 140,103,90 --touch 97,68,113, plus --skip on the same-pink neck shadow between the collar). Check faces, necks and open mouths afterwards; restore them from the source if hit.
